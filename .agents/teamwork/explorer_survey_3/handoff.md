> **Core Summary**: Deep read-only audit of all Silo components (`app/`, `daemon/`, `module/`, `scripts/`, `.github/workflows/build.yml`, `README.md`) identified **30 concrete bugs/mismatches/security flaws** (7 Critical, 12 High, 8 Medium, 3 Low) across App ↔ Daemon ↔ Android Framework ↔ KernelSU Module boundaries. ## 4. Conclusion

1. **Direct Answer to the User's Question ("apa ini batasannya?")**:
   - **Unresponsive / unclickable permissions & Monitor buttons**: **NOT an Android limitation** — caused by Silo's single-mutex blocking IPC (`SiloClient.kt` non-cancellable `readLine()` + 15s bridge timeout mismatch), `silod` holding `state_mtx` across 9+ synchronous `settings`/`dumpsys` subprocesses on every 3–4s poll, 11 sequential `dumpsys appops --op` calls, hidden error snackbars behind `ModalBottomSheet`, tiny ~24dp pill touch targets, and concurrent revoke+refresh races (`CB-04`, `CB-05`).
   - **"Saat dicabut malah diizinkan" & Screenshot Contradiction (`DICABUT` + `Normal` green, `Palsukan` + `Cabut` both lit)**: **NOT an Android limitation** — caused by `parse_appops_get_output` blindly overwriting `Uid mode: ignore` with package-level `allow`/`default` (`CB-01`), `set_app_permission_action` omitting `--uid` mode writes and silently swallowing stdout/`pm revoke` failures (`CB-02`), broken pill/badge mutual-exclusion logic in `AppsScreen.kt` (`CB-02`), and `AppInventory.kt` counting non-revocable special perms (`SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`) as granted runtime perms (`CB-09`).
   - **`Kunci Blokir` waiting before force-stopping & revoking**: **Half Silo bug, half Android platform behavior**:
     - The **15–20 second delay** is a **Silo bug** (`scopes.rs:568` 15s periodic sweep inside 5s loop + missing `user-fixed`/`policy-fixed` permission flags to block re-grant popups in the first place — `CB-03`).
     - The **force-stop (`killUid`)** when revoking a runtime permission from an *already-running* app IS an Android OS security behavior (`PermissionManagerService`), whereas AppOps `ignore`/`deny` blocks access without killing the process — AND preventing re-grants proactively via `user-fixed` + `--uid` AppOps eliminates surprise force-stops during normal app usage!
